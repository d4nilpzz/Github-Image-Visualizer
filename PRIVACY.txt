Privacy Policy - GitHub Image Visualizer

Last updated: September 11, 2026

GitHub Image Visualizer does not collect, store, or share any personal or user data.

What the extension does:
- It runs only on pages of github.com.
- It reads the file list shown on the page to find image files.
- It loads those images directly from GitHub's servers (raw.githubusercontent.com) to show them as thumbnails.

What the extension does NOT do:
- It does not collect personal information, browsing history, or any other data.
- It does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking.
- It does not store anything on your device or on any server.
- It does not send any data to the developer or to third parties.

Image requests go only to GitHub, so they are covered by GitHub's own privacy policy.

Changes:
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address.
